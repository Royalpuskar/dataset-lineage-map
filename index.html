A baseline is a reference, not a rebuild.

Recording a baseline accepts the current artifacts as your new reference. It does not regenerate derived data. Inspect each affected artifact and rerun your real pipeline before accepting its results. Exported hashes detect differences against that reference; they do not prove who created it.
